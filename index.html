I'm a graduate AI researcher at the University of Edinburgh, specialising in Robotics and Reinforcement Learning. My undergraduate degree in Engineering Physics gave me a rigorous foundation in mathematics, signal processing, and dynamical systems — which I now apply to teaching robots how to move, perceive, and make decisions.

I'm drawn to embodied intelligence — agents that must act in the physical world under uncertainty. From processing satellite data at ISRO to computational ocean analysis at IIT Delhi, I've always worked at the edge of computation and physical systems. Robotics is where those worlds meet permanently.

MSc Dissertation · ICRA · CoRL
00 — University of Edinburgh, 2026
No Eyes, No Problem: What Does Touch Contribute to Blind Baoding Ball Manipulation?
A reinforcement-learning policy that rotates two Baoding balls on a Shadow Dexterous Hand Lite using only proprioception and binary touch — no cameras. Trained entirely in simulation and transferred zero-shot to the real hand. Ablations show tactile feedback is what keeps the rotation going: remove it and sustained Baoding collapses.
112.5rotations on hardware
16tactile sensors (12 FSR + 4 BioTac)
0cameras · fine-tuning
My contributions: designed the tactile sensor layout and fitted the FSRs to the physical hand; Slew-rate Domain Randomisation (SlewDR) in sim and as the hardware rate limit; per-channel hysteresis tactile calibration; implemented Stuck-AT Taxel randomisation; trained the headline tactile policy.
