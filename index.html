/* background */

I'm a Research Assistant in the Statistical Machine Learning and Motor Control (SLMC) group at IPAB, University of Edinburgh, focused on robot learning, dexterous manipulation, tactile sensing and sim-to-real reinforcement learning. My background in AI and Engineering Physics brings together mathematics, signal processing and dynamical systems. I apply that foundation to teaching robots how to move, perceive and make decisions.

/* what drives me */

I'm drawn to embodied intelligence: agents that act in the physical world under uncertainty. From processing satellite data at ISRO to computational ocean analysis at IIT Delhi, I've always worked at the edge of computation and physical systems. Robotics is where those worlds meet permanently.

I've built web apps, competed in science hackathons (Top 20 nationwide in XR-tech), and led teams. I believe the best engineers are curious generalists who execute with precision.

MSc Dissertation · ICRA · CoRL
00 · University of Edinburgh, 2026
No Eyes, No Problem: What Does Touch Contribute to Blind Baoding Ball Manipulation?
A reinforcement-learning policy rotates two Baoding balls on a Shadow Dexterous Hand Lite using proprioception and binary touch, without cameras. Sensor-aligned simulation, tactile calibration and domain randomisation enable zero-shot transfer to the real hand. The project studies what touch contributes to sustained manipulation and which regions of the hand matter most.
112.5peak full rotations on hardware
34.75mean full rotations · 10 trials
16tactile sensors · 12 FSR + 4 BioTac
60 HzPPO policy deployment
Method & dissertation details

The tactile system combines 12 FSR sensors and four BioTac fingertips with per-channel hysteresis calibration. PadTac USD assets in Isaac Lab, a ROS deployment pipeline and slew-rate domain randomisation account for sensing and hardware motion limits. PPO policies transfer zero-shot to the physical hand.

MSc dissertation, Jan-Sept 2026: Development of a Low-cost Tactile System for Dexterous Manipulation and Investigating the Sim-To-Real Gap in Tactile Reinforcement Learning.

Supervision: Prof. Sethu Vijayakumar and Elle Miller.

Authors: Ayush Deshmukh*, Nalin Agarwal*, Elle Miller, Sethu Vijayakumar.
*Equal contribution.

LONG-TERM GOAL · EXPLORATORY

Consciousness &
self-awareness.

My long-term goal is to build embodied systems that could develop subjective experience and a sense of self. I want to understand how consciousness and self-awareness could emerge through perception, memory and action.

I want to explore agents that build a model of themselves, reflect on what they know and learn through experience in the physical world. Robotics gives me a way to connect those questions to perception, action and learning.
